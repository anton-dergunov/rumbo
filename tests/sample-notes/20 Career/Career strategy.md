## The short answer

Look for **hard technical problems** and a strong engineering culture, then decide by the end of the quarter. See [[Project Ideas]] for candidates and [[AI Systems#Evaluation|how to evaluate them]].

> [!tip] Rule of thumb
> Prefer the role where you would learn the most in the first year.

## Score

The expected value is $E = \sum_i p_i v_i$, where $p_i$ is the chance of outcome $i$ and $v_i$ its value. A price of $5 and $10 stays text.

$$
\text{score} = \frac{\text{learning} + \text{impact}}{2}
$$
